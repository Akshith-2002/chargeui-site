2Who can use ChargeUI

You must be at least 18 years old and able to enter into a binding contract under the Indian Contract Act, 1872. If you use the Services on behalf of a company or fleet, you confirm that you are authorised to accept these Terms for it.

3Your account and sign-in

  • You sign in with an Indian mobile number and a one-time password (OTP) sent by SMS. There is no password to set or remember.
  • On first sign-in you complete your profile: name, date of birth, gender and email address. Adding a GST number is optional.
  • You can browse stations, prices and reviews without an account. We ask you to sign in only when you start charging.
  • Your sign-in stays active while you use the app and lapses after 7 days without use, after which you sign in again with an OTP.
  • Keep your phone and SIM secure. You are responsible for activity on your account, including sessions started with RFID cards or vehicles linked to it. Tell us straight away at support@chargeui.in if you think someone else has used your account.

4Charging sessions

  • Stations are run by ChargeUI or by partner operators and franchise owners on the ChargeUI network, including roaming partners. The operator is shown on the station page and on the map.
  • You choose how much to charge by rupee amount or by energy (kWh). Before you start, the app shows an estimate of energy, cost and time. Estimates depend on your vehicle, the charger and conditions on site, and are not guaranteed.
  • A session stops when your chosen amount or energy is reached, when you stop it in the app, when you unplug, or when the charger or vehicle ends it.
  • At sites with load management, the power available to each vehicle may change during a session so the site stays within its sanctioned electrical load. This can make charging slower than the charger's maximum rating.
  • Charging is start-on-arrival. We do not currently offer reservations. "Notify me" alerts for busy connectors are a courtesy and do not hold a charger for you.
Your vehicle is your responsibility. ChargeUI does not check whether your vehicle or battery can accept the energy you select. Make sure the connector and charging power suit your vehicle, follow the manufacturer's instructions and the instructions on the charger, and do not leave a session unattended in a way that is unsafe.

5Prices, wallet and payments

  • Tariffs are set per connector by the station operator and shown in the app per kWh, plus 18% GST, before you start. The tariff that applies is the one shown when your session starts.
  • Charging is paid from your ChargeUI wallet. You add money through our payment partner using UPI, cards or net-banking, as the payment partner supports.
  • When a session starts, the amount you selected is held from your wallet. At the end of the session you are charged only for the energy delivered, with GST, and the rest of the hold is released back to your wallet automatically.
  • A GST invoice is available for every completed session. If you add your GSTIN, invoices carry it.
  • Wallet balance is not interest-bearing, cannot be transferred to another user and can be used only for services on the ChargeUI network. Any limits that apply to stored balances under Reserve Bank of India rules apply to your wallet.

6Refunds and cancellation

  • If a session starts but no energy is delivered, it is marked failed and the full amount held is returned to your wallet.
  • Unused amounts from completed sessions are returned to your wallet automatically when the session ends.
  • If you think you were charged incorrectly, raise a ticket in the app (Help and support) or write to support@chargeui.in within 30 days of the session. We review the charger's meter records and, if an error is confirmed, credit the difference to your wallet.
  • To withdraw unused wallet balance to your original payment method, write to support@chargeui.in from your registered email. We process eligible requests within 7 working days, less any charges our payment partner deducts.
  • Because charging is start-on-arrival, there is nothing to cancel before a session starts. You can stop a running session at any time and pay only for the energy delivered.

7RFID cards and Autocharge

  • You can link RFID cards to your account and use them to start sessions on supported chargers. Sessions started with a card are billed to the linked wallet or, for fleet cards, to the group's billing account.
  • If a card is lost, block it in the app straight away. You are responsible for sessions started with a card before you block it.
  • Where Autocharge or Plug & Charge is available and you switch it on, plugging in a linked vehicle starts a session and bills your wallet without opening the app. You can switch it off or remove the vehicle link at any time.

8Using stations responsibly

  • Use chargers only as intended and follow any on-site instructions, signage and parking rules.
  • Move your vehicle when charging ends. Operators may apply idle or parking fees, which are shown in the app where they apply.
  • Do not damage, tamper with or misuse chargers, cables or site equipment, and report any fault or hazard through the app.
  • Reviews and photos you post must be honest and lawful and must not include other people's personal information. We may remove content that breaks these rules.
  • Do not misuse the Services, for example by attempting to access other accounts, interfering with the apps or chargers, or using the Services for anything unlawful.
