Questions drivers ask

Do I need an account to find a charger?

No. Browse the map, stations, prices and reviews as a guest. We ask for your phone number only when you tap Start charging, then bring you back to the same connector.

How do I sign in?

With your 10-digit mobile number and a 6-digit OTP by SMS. There's no password to remember.

Why rupees or kWh, and not battery %?

Chargers measure energy, not your battery. Pick an amount or a number of units, and the session stops when it's reached, when you stop it, or when you unplug.

What happens to the money held from my wallet?

You pay only for the energy delivered. The rest goes back to your wallet automatically when the session ends. If no energy was delivered at all, the whole amount comes back.

Can I book a charger in advance?

Not yet. Charging is start-on-arrival. Tap Notify me on a busy connector to hear when it frees up.

Does it work for scooters and bikes?

Yes. Add your two-wheeler under My vehicles and use the Two-wheeler filter on the map to find LEV connectors.
