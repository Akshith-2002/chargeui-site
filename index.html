Dynamic load management for AC chargers

More chargers on the same electrical connection, without tripping the main breaker.

Gated communities usually have a fixed sanctioned load. Six 7.4 kW chargers need 44 kW if every one runs flat out, which most apartment connections can't supply. ChargeUI shares the available power across the cars that are actually plugged in, and rebalances the moment one arrives or leaves.

  • Configurable maximum site load
  • Power shared in real time across connected chargers
  • Round-robin allocation and dual-gun charger support
  • More charging points without upgrading the transformer

Depends on charger compatibility, site wiring, metering and integration.

Issue tracking, from fault to fixed

No scattered WhatsApp messages, forgotten complaints or manual follow-ups.

When a charger reports a fault, ChargeUI opens a ticket, sets its priority and assigns it to the right technician. The owner is kept informed until it's closed. A charge point that stays unavailable for 10 minutes alerts the station owner, and repeats every 30 minutes until it's fixed.

  • Tickets created automatically from charger alarms
  • Categorised, prioritised and assigned
  • In-app, email and WhatsApp updates
  • Full service history for every charger

Questions businesses ask

What is ChargeUI?

An EV charging technology and infrastructure company. We provide charging management software (CMS), white-label platforms, a driver app and end-to-end installation services.

How does load management help a gated community?

Instead of reserving full power for every charger, ChargeUI shares your sanctioned load across the cars that are actually charging and rebalances as they come and go. You can install more chargers on the same connection. It depends on compatible chargers and site metering.

Can I launch my own charging brand?

Yes. White-label partners get the CMS and driver app with their own name, logo and colours, plus partner and franchise management.

How do daily settlements work?

Each day's charging revenue is calculated per franchise owner, with payment processing and platform fees deducted, and a statement showing the net amount. The exact fees follow your commercial agreement.

Does it work with chargers from different manufacturers?

The platform works with OCPP 1.6J and 2.0.1 chargers. Actual compatibility depends on the manufacturer, firmware and protocol implementation, so we check each model first.

Can I manage stations in several cities?

Yes. All stations and charge points are monitored and managed from one login, with separate access for franchise partners.

Do you install chargers too?

Yes: site survey, power approvals, HT/LT electrical works, cabling, AC and DC charger installation, civil works, testing and commissioning.
