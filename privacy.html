1Who we are and what this covers

This privacy policy explains how ChargeUI Private Limited ("ChargeUI", "we", "us") collects, uses and protects personal data when you use the ChargeUI website, the user app and charging services, and when businesses use the ChargeUI CMS. We follow the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 ("DPDP Act") and the Information Technology Act, 2000.

For people using the user app and our website, ChargeUI decides how personal data is used (we are the "data fiduciary"). Where a franchise owner or operator runs stations on the ChargeUI platform, they also receive session data for their own stations, as explained below.

2Data we collect

AccountMobile number, name, date of birth, gender, email address, and optionally GST number and business name.
VehiclesVehicle type, make, model and registration number, and for Autocharge the identifier your vehicle reports to the charger.
ChargingStations and connectors used, start and end times, energy delivered, amounts held, charged and refunded, invoices, and session events from the charger.
PaymentsWallet balance and transaction history. Card, UPI and bank details are handled by our payment partner; we do not store full card numbers.
RFID cardsCard numbers you link or that are assigned to you, and sessions started with them.
LocationYour device location, only if you allow it, to show nearby stations and directions. You can use the app without it.
Support and reviewsTickets and messages you send us, and reviews, ratings and photos you post.
Device and usageApp version, device type, push notification token, crash logs and how you use the app.
Business contactsIf you enquire through our website or use the CMS: name, company, phone, email, city and your message.

3How we use your data

  • To create and secure your account and sign you in with an OTP.
  • To find stations, start and stop sessions, manage load at sites, and show live progress.
  • To hold, charge and refund wallet amounts and issue GST invoices.
  • To send notifications about your sessions, refunds and account, and announcements you can turn off.
  • To provide support, investigate faults and resolve complaints.
  • To keep the Services safe, prevent fraud and misuse, and meet legal, tax and accounting duties.
  • To understand usage in aggregate and improve the Services.

We process your data on the basis of your consent, which you give when you sign up and when you allow features such as location or notifications, and for legitimate uses permitted by the DPDP Act, such as complying with law. We do not sell your personal data.

4Who we share it with

  • Station operators and franchise owners: details of sessions at their stations (time, connector, energy, amount and vehicle or card identifier) so they can run and settle their business. They receive your name only where needed to resolve a complaint.
  • Roaming partners: the data needed to start, bill and support a session when you charge on their network.
  • Service providers working for us: payment processing, SMS and email delivery, push notifications, maps, cloud hosting and customer support tools. They may use the data only to provide their service to us.
  • Authorities: when required by law, a court order or a lawful request from a government agency.
  • A buyer or successor, if ChargeUI is involved in a merger or acquisition, under the same protections.

5How long we keep it

We keep account data while your account is open. Charging, payment and invoice records are kept for at least 8 years to meet tax and accounting laws. Support tickets are kept for 3 years. Location is used when you request it and is not stored with your history.

When data is no longer needed, we delete it or make it anonymous.

6Your rights and choices

  • See a summary of the personal data we hold about you and how it is used.
  • Correct or update your details. Most can be edited in Profile.
  • Ask us to delete your data and close your account, subject to records we must keep by law.
  • Withdraw consent, for example by turning off location, notifications or Autocharge. Some features may stop working.
  • Nominate a person to exercise your rights if you die or become unable to do so.
  • Raise a grievance with us and, if unresolved, with the Data Protection Board of India.

To use any of these rights, use Help and support in the app or write to privacy@chargeui.in from your registered email or phone. We respond within 30 days.

7How we protect your data

  • Data is encrypted in transit and stored with access controls on cloud infrastructure.
  • Staff and operators can see only the data their role needs, and access is logged.
  • Sign-in uses one-time passwords and sessions expire after inactivity.
  • Payments are handled by a PCI-DSS compliant payment partner.

No system is completely secure. If a breach affects your personal data, we will inform you and the Data Protection Board as the law requires.

8Children

The Services are meant for adults. We do not knowingly collect personal data from anyone under 18. If you think a child has given us data, write to privacy@chargeui.in and we will delete it.

9Cookies and similar technologies

Our website uses only the storage it needs to work, such as remembering preferences in your browser. If we add analytics or advertising cookies, we will update this policy and ask for your consent where required.

10Where data is stored

We store data on servers in India where possible. If a service provider processes data outside India, we do so only to countries not restricted by the Government of India and with safeguards in our contracts.

11Changes to this policy

We may update this policy. We will post the new version here with a new "Last updated" date and tell you in the app or by email about significant changes.

12Contact and grievance officer

You can reach the Grievance Officer of ChargeUI Private Limited at privacy@chargeui.in. We acknowledge grievances within 48 hours and aim to resolve them within 30 days.

Questions about this policy?Write to privacy@chargeui.in. We reply within 7 working days.
